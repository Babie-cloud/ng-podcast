Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 May 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ng-podcast (“we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, discloses, and protects information when you use our website and related podcast, writing, and storytelling services (the “Service”).

1. Who is responsible?

For the purposes applicable data protection laws, ng-podcast acts as the data controller for personal data processed through this Service, unless stated otherwise for a specific processor relationship (such as strictly on behalf of enterprise customers).

2. Categories of personal data

Depending on how you interact with us, we may process:

  • Account & login data: name, email address, password hash, profile details you choose, timestamps, identifiers.
  • Publisher & creative content: podcast metadata, descriptions, uploads (including audio/writing/story files), optional captions/transcripts where you submit them.
  • Usage data: pages visited, approximate device/language, referrer, timestamps, coarse interaction events (listening or navigation where measured).
  • Technical & diagnostics: IP address or truncated IP logs, cookies or similar identifiers, client type, diagnostic logs strictly needed for security/reliability.
  • Communications: messages or support inquiries you submit to us.

3. Why we process data & legal bases

Depending on jurisdiction, we rely on one or more of the following lawful bases:

  • Contract: to provide the Service you request (authentication, uploads, playlists, dashboards).
  • Legitimate interests: to secure the Service, prevent abuse/fraud, improve performance, prioritize product development, analyse aggregate usage metrics, defend legal claims — balanced against your fundamental rights and freedoms.
  • Legal obligation: where law requires retaining or disclosing records.
  • Consent: where mandated (non-essential cookies/marketing integrations, newsletters if ever opt-in). Withdraw consent anytime where applicable via settings or unsubscribing.

4. Disclosure of personal data

We disclose personal data only as needed:

  • Service providers (processors) that host servers, CDN, transcription if enabled separately, email delivery/transactional notices, observability/logging, customer support tooling — pursuant to contractual data protection clauses.
  • Authorities & law enforcement when required by law or to protect vital interests and the rights of others where proportionate.
  • Business transfers (merger/asset sale) subject to safeguarding obligations comparable to those here.
  • Public-facing content: what you knowingly publish via the Service (e.g., public podcast/show pages) is visible accordingly.

5. Retention

We retain data only while needed for stated purposes plus applicable legal record-keeping. Account data persists while the account stays active unless you request deletion consistent with lawful exceptions. Diagnostics logs may rotate on short cycles. Deleted content backups may linger temporarily in encrypted snapshots controlled by safeguards.

6. Cookies & similar technologies

We may use strictly necessary cookies (session/auth or security preferences) plus optional analytics or preference cookies consistent with banners where required. Manage choices through browser settings / any cookie UI we expose. Third-party providers may set cookies — review their notices.

7. Children

The Service is not directed to individuals under the age of 13 (or higher thresholds where applicable). We do not knowingly The Service is not aimed at minors under thresholds defined by regional law without verifiable parental/guardian consent regimes. If we learn we unknowingly processed such data inconsistently with law, we will delete it promptly.

8. Cross-border transfers

Data may transit or be hosted in jurisdictions other than yours. Where required, we implement appropriate safeguards, including standard contractual clauses or equivalent lawful transfer mechanisms supplemented by supplementary measures as appropriate after assessment.

9. Security

We adopt reasonable technical & organisational measures (encryption in transit where supported, segregated credentials, access limitation, patching). Absolute security cannot be guaranteed; safeguard your passwords and endpoints.

10. Automated decisions & profiling

We do not use fully automated decisions with legal/similar significantly affecting you purely by algorithm absent human review. Low-level scoring for abuse prevention may combine signals but is calibrated conservatively — contact us to discuss rights if you believe erroneous enforcement occurred.

11. Regional rights summary

  • European Economic Area, UK & Switzerland alignment: access, rectify, erase, restrict, object where applicable, portability of machine-readable data you supplied, lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.
  • California (CCPA / CPRA): residents may exercise rights described in CPRA disclosures (know, delete where allowed, correction, limit use of sensitive PI in defined contexts, nondiscrimination) subject to exemptions.
  • Others: local consumer laws may confer similar rights—you may designate an authorized agent when permitted subject to verification.

12. Exercising your rights

Contact us via the mechanisms published on the Service. We may authenticate requests reasonably to safeguard accounts. We respond within timelines prescribed by applicable law unless complex law demands extension with notice where required. If dissatisfied, escalate to supervisory authority or regulators for your jurisdiction.
